One of the most important and influential Techno tracks of the nineties presented with all remixes - the great, the ok and the ugly - in one set for the first time ! Also included is the unreleased Analogue Mix and the Omnichord Version, available here for the very first time ! But still the original is unrivaled and holds the energy and atmosphere of it's heyday :-)

Thomas P. Heckmann Mainz, Germany

Keyfigure of early and contemporary german Techno, EBM-Techno and Acid, Thomas P. Heckmann has shaped the landscape of electronic music since the very beginning !
